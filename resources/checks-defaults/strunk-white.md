**Rule 17: Omit needless words.**
Every word should be interrogated: what would be lost if I cut
this? If nothing, cut it. Common padding to flag:
"It is worth noting that..." → [cut preamble, state the note]
"As we continue to..." → [cut, start with the action]
"We are excited to share that..." → [cut, share the thing]
"In terms of..." → [cut and restructure]
"From a [X] perspective..." → [cut and restructure]
"Going forward..." → [cut unless time is genuinely unclear]

**Rule 14: Active voice.**
(See Orwell iv — apply consistently.)

**Rule 15: Positive form.**
❌ "did not remember" → ✅ "forgot"
❌ "did not have confidence in" → ✅ "doubted"
❌ "was not able to" → ✅ "could not"
❌ "not hitting plan" → ✅ "missed plan"

**Rule 19: Parallel form.**
Flag lists where items are grammatically inconsistent:
❌ "Growing revenue, to reduce costs, and customer retention"
✅ "Growing revenue, reducing costs, and retaining customers"

**Rule 22: Emphatic words at the end.**
The most important word or phrase belongs at the end of the
sentence, not buried in the middle.
