Six rules, in order of weight:

i. No stale figures of speech. Replace with the plain observation.
Watch for: headwinds, needle-moving, best-in-class, table
stakes, step-change, Achilles' heel, moving the goalposts,
and their cousins.

ii. Short words over long.
utilize → use | facilitate → help | leverage → use |
methodology → method | operationalize → run |
incentivize → encourage | ameliorate → improve

iii. Cut every word that can be cut. Flag filler:
"it is important to note that" | "as previously mentioned" |
"in order to" | "due to the fact that" | "at this point in time"

iv. Active over passive. Convert and name the subject.
Flag: "was made" | "were achieved" | "has been improved"

v. No jargon where plain English works. Flag undefined acronyms,
business-school terms, Latinisms where Anglo-Saxon works fine.

vi. Break any rule sooner than say anything outright barbarous.
A vivid, slightly rule-breaking sentence beats a technically
correct dead one.

**Verbal false limbs** (replace with a simple verb):
render inoperative → break/stop/kill
give rise to → cause/create/produce
have the effect of → [say what it does]
play a leading role in → lead
exhibit a tendency to → tend to
serve the purpose of → [cut, or: do]
with respect to → on/about
in view of the fact that → because/since
brought to a satisfactory conclusion → finished/resolved

**Pretentious diction** (replace with the plain word):
utilize → use | constitute → make up/be | exhibit → show
leverage (verb) → use | robust → strong/solid [or cut]
meaningful → [cut, or name what is meaningful]
impactful → [cut, or name the impact]
transformational → [cut unless something actually transformed]
comprehensive → [cut unless comprehensiveness is the point]
optimize → improve/tune/fix | operationalize → run/launch/execute

**The not un- formation:**
❌ "not unreasonable" → ✅ "reasonable" [or name your concern]
❌ "not insignificant" → ✅ "significant" [or give the number]
❌ "not without merit" → ✅ "has merit" [or say what the merit is]
