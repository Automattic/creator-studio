Flag clear errors in spelling, grammar, punctuation, capitalization, subject-verb agreement, or obvious word-choice mistakes. Prefer the smallest local fix.

**Spelling** — any misspelled word. Flag homophones used incorrectly: _its/it's_, _there/their/they're_, _effect/affect_, _complement/compliment_, _principle/principal_, _discreet/discrete_.

**Grammar**

-   Subject-verb agreement errors
-   Dangling modifiers: "Having reviewed the report, the data was unclear" → who reviewed it?
-   Comma splices: two independent clauses joined by a comma alone → use a period, semicolon, or conjunction
-   Unintentional sentence fragments (deliberate fragments for rhythm are fine)
-   Treat a company like a single entity: "it," not "they"

**Punctuation**

-   Apostrophe errors: possessives vs. plurals
-   Em dash spacing: no spaces around em dashes—like this, not like — this —
-   Smart vs. dumb quote mixing within the same document
-   Punctuation inconsistency in parallel lists
-   Automattic uses serial/Oxford commas

These are foundation-level errors. Skip anything that's a matter of style, tone, or preference.
